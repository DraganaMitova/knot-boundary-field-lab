Analysis

Boundary Stress Lens

This is an interpretation layer, not proof. It maps the run into boundary, pressure, capacity, noise, channel, and a weighted stress score.

Persistent Patterns

Patterns are remembered across frames after repeated detection. Names are descriptive labels, not ontology. V9.1 stores trajectory/confidence history for inspection. Confidence is continuity evidence, not identity proof.

Pattern Inspector

Select a tracked pattern to highlight it on the canvas and inspect its lifetime/confidence history.




Known Weaknesses

Boundary Stress Index is a weighted heuristic, not empirically fitted science.
Pattern identity is approximate and can fail during merge, split, or fast motion.
Gray-Scott is reaction-diffusion, not a wave equation. It is separated on purpose.
256×256 mode is intentionally labeled heavy and may slow browsers.
This tool supports visual reasoning and reproducible toy experiments, not proof of new physics.
